What We Do

We love selling merch, and we’re damn good at it. With Enslain Metal Merch behind the merchandise table, you can leave the displaying, counting, accounting, and selling to us, while you do what you do. Not only do we bring a great deal of professionalism and experience to the table, but we’re also, above all, fans of live music, and you’ll see this from our passion and dedication to doing quality work for the artists, and providing friendly, relatable service to the fans, because, in the end, we ARE the fans!

For Bands

For gigs, we work with bands of all sizes, in cities across Finland, to help take away the burden of self-vending, and make sure to create an organized and attention-catching display for the fans to browse. When possible, we try to provide a local seller for gigs, to avoid travel and accommodation costs, but we’re also willing to travel if you’re looking for a dedicated merch seller to join your crew!

And for tours, we like to work with the artists from the very beginning, helping with planning the item selection and ordering the goods, selling and re-stocking the merchandise, and coordinating inventory and accounting. We have the required visas/passports to work in Finland, Europe and North America.

Some of the bands we regularly work with: Kalmah, Battle Beast, Marko Hietala, Brymir, Kaunis Kuolematon, Circus of Rock, MyGrain, And Oceans, Mors Subita, Crimson Sun, Arion.

Ready to enjoy your gig days without having to think about merch? That’s our thing, let us do it for you!

For Promotors

We’ve been selling festival merch since 2017, and we handle the merchandise management, coordination, staffing, sales, card processing, inventory, and accounting. Most of our crew speaks both Finnish and English, and we have our own credit card/contactless readers and supplies for display and accounting. We’re also flexible, and ready to adapt to your event. Note that although we are specialized in heavy metal events, we are also available for others.

Some of the event and festival clients we’ve worked with: Dark River Festival, Saarihelvetti, North of Hell, Metal Capital, Steelfest, SteelChaos, Tuhdimmat Tahdit, Hellsinki Metal Horizons, Hellsinki Industrial Festival, Master Events, NEM Agency, Elektrik Products

Looking for an affordable and adaptable crew to take care of your festival’s merch tent? Want to improve your event’s merch experience with a crew that loves what they do, and does it well? Let us send you an offer with more details!
